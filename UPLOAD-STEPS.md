# UPLOAD STEPS — The Gatekeeper v1.0 (owner's taps)

The pack is checked and frozen. These are the taps only the owner can
make, in the Muzzle pattern.

1. On github.com, create a new public repository named
   **The-Gatekeeper**. Do not add a README from GitHub's side — the
   pack carries its own.
2. In the repo's About (gear icon), paste the fun line from
   STORE-PAGE.txt, exactly:
   "Your router is the gate at the property line between your home
   and the wild world. This guide helps you hang a new one — with
   your own hands, and your own key."
3. Upload the contents of the the-gatekeeper folder (README.md,
   STORE-PAGE.txt, EXPERIENCE-NOTES.md, LICENSE, UPLOAD-STEPS.md,
   gatekeeper.ico, gatekeeper-icon-1024.png, and the kit/ folder
   with its six files).
4. Create the release: tag **v1.0**, title "The Gatekeeper 1.0".
   In the release, drag **the-gatekeeper-1.0.zip** into the
   ATTACH BINARIES box — not the notes box (a notes-drop makes
   only a link, learned at Muzzle's publish).
5. Release notes (short form, honest): "First release. The
   field-proven E8450 guide in kit form — no graphical window
   yet, said plainly in the README. One router walked and
   receipted; everything beyond it is labeled in theory."
6. Publish. Then the standing check: open the public page signed
   out and read it as a stranger would.
